## III. Background

Computing is not just "programming." As introduced in the Unit 2
lecture, the field branches into several distinct career pathways, each
with its own operational scope, required technical competencies,
representative job titles, and professional standard or reference body:
Software Engineering, Systems & Cloud Infrastructure, Data Science & AI
Engineering, Cybersecurity & Threat Defense, and Information Systems &
Enterprise IT. Each pathway also has its own career roadmap --- the
entry-level, mid-level, senior-level, and expert/leadership roles a
professional typically grows through. This activity asks you to review
those five pathways, choose the one that interests you most, and map out
what growing in that pathway could look like for you.
